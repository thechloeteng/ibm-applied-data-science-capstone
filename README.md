# IBM Applied Data Science Capstone

**SpaceX** has significantly reduced the cost of rocket launches by developing reusable Falcon 9 rockets. The company advertises Falcon 9 launches at approximately **$62 million**, while traditional launch providers may charge over **$165 million** per launch.

A major factor behind this cost advantage is **SpaceX's** ability to **recover and reuse the Falcon 9 first stage**. However, the success of first-stage landing depends on various mission factors, such as launch site, payload, orbit type, and booster characteristics.

In this project, we analyse historical Falcon 9 launch data and apply machine learning techniques to **predict whether the first stage will successfully land.** The results can help estimate launch costs and provide insights for companies seeking to compete in the commercial space launch market.

1. **Can we predict whether the Falcon 9 first stage will successfully land based on historical launch data?**
2. **Which factors have the greatest impact on first-stage landing success?**
3. **What launch conditions or configurations provide the highest probability of a successful landing?**
4. **Can the prediction model help estimate launch costs and support decision-making for companies competing with SpaceX?**
